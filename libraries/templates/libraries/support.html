Support Bookworm

Bookworm is a free, community-built map of Little Free Libraries across British Columbia. It has no paywalls and no third-party ads, and it never sells data. It is built and run by one person, Ben.

Keeping it online costs about $250 a year for hosting, the database and the domain. Anything beyond that goes toward QR stickers for library stewards, community library walks, and mapping more of BC.

Other ways to help

Money is the smallest part of it. Add a library you know, refresh one with a new shelfie photo, or share Bookworm with a neighbour. Updates live on Instagram (opens in a new tab).

Bookworm is not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible and no receipts can be issued.

Donations never buy a listing, a ranking, or any say over what appears on the map.
