Free Little Library Walk

Sunday, August 16, 2026·10:00am - 3:00pm


A friendly, unhurried procession visiting Little Free Libraries across Central Vancouver. Bring a few books to share, trade, or leave along the way.

What to expect

A slow walk between little libraries

Inspired by Jane's Walk, this is a relaxed afternoon spent wandering from one Little Free Library to the next with neighbours. Leave a book if there's room, take one if it catches your eye, snap a shelfie so the next person knows what's inside, and help us discover libraries that aren't on the map yet. There's no set pace and no pressure -- come for one stop or stay for all of them.

What to bring

  • A few books to pass on (only leave one if there's room)
  • Water -- there are refill points along the way
  • Sun protection: hat, sunscreen, shade-friendly clothes
  • Comfortable shoes for a few easy kilometres

How it runs

We start and finish at VPL Mount Pleasant. Rest stops are come-and-go, so you can peel off or join back in whenever suits you.

We've scheduled the walk to avoid the peak afternoon heat. If it turns extreme, we may postpone or cancel and will email everyone who registered.

Families welcome. Kids 16 and below must be accompanied by an adult.
If turnout is high, we may split into two groups for part of the route and finish at a second spot nearby -- we'll sort out the details and announce them at the start.
The route
