What is Bookworm?

Bookworm is a free community map of every Little Free Library in British Columbia. Stewards opt in; readers can find them, see what's on the shelves, and add their own photos. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Open the map →

Funded by a Neighbourhood Small Grant through the Vancouver Foundation, administered by Mount Pleasant and Little Mountain Neighbourhood Houses.
